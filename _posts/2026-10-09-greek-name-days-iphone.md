In Greece, many people celebrate their name day, the feast of the saint they are named after, as much as their birthday, if not more. The iPhone calendar shows birthdays, but not name days. If you have Greek friends, family or colleagues, that's an easy way to miss one.

## The usual way: subscribe to a name day calendar

There are ready-made name day calendars you can subscribe to from the Calendar settings: **Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar**. They work, but with two problems:

- They show **every** name of every day, not which of your own people are celebrating.
- **Movable** name days, which depend on Orthodox Easter, are often wrong or missing.

## With Chronos: only your own people

[Chronos](/en/chronos/) knows both fixed and movable Greek name days and matches them with your contacts. In the morning it sends a notification with who is celebrating today, at a time you choose, and an optional second reminder later in the day.

It also checks the "Company" field of each contact, because many people save friends under a nickname, with their real name stored elsewhere.

The **On this day** button shows the day's name days and historical events, with a red badge for how many of your contacts are celebrating.

If your iPhone isn't set to Greek, switch on the Greece features in the app's settings.

## A small tip

For name days to be found, your contacts need their first name in the "First name" field. If "Maria Papadopoulou" is stored entirely as a last name, fix it once in Contacts.
