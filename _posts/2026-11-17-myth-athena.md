All over the world, the owl stands for wisdom. The reason is Greek: it was Athena's bird.

## Athena in Greek myth

Athena was born from the head of Zeus, fully grown and armed. The goddess of wisdom, crafts and strategic thinking, she was the protector of Athens. The owl and the olive tree are her symbols.

## The owl, from ancient coins to today

- Athens put the goddess's owl on its silver **tetradrachms**: Athena's head on one side, the owl with an olive sprig on the other. They were among the best known coins of the ancient world.
- Greece kept the same design for its own **1 euro coin**, which reproduces the owl of an Athenian tetradrachm.
- Even the little owl's scientific name is **Athene noctua**: the genus Athene is named after the goddess.
