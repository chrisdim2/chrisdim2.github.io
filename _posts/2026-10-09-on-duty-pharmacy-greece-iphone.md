It's Sunday evening, you're on holiday in Greece, and the pharmacy round the corner is closed. Greek pharmacies take turns staying open at night, at weekends and on holidays, but which one is it today? Maps often show "Closed", and the notice on the pharmacy door doesn't help if you're not standing in front of it.

Here are three ways, from fastest to slowest.

## 1. With an app that knows the rota

[Chronos](/en/chronos/) shows the pharmacies on duty near you, on a map and in a list, with distance, hours and phone number. One tap opens directions in Maps. The data comes from each region's pharmacists' association, and the area is picked automatically from your location. You can also choose another region by hand.

On the same screen you'll find the hospitals on call in Attica, Thessaloniki, Achaia and Crete, including which clinics are on duty today.

If your iPhone isn't set to Greek, switch on the Greece features in the app's settings.

## 2. On the local pharmacists' association website

Every region in Greece has a pharmacists' association, and most of them publish the day's rota on their website. Search Safari for "εφημερεύοντα φαρμακεία" (on-duty pharmacies) followed by the name of the town.

- In Safari, tap the share button and choose **Add to Home Screen**. Next time, the page opens with one tap.
- Check the date on the page: some sites switch to the next day's list in the morning, others at midday.

## 3. On the pharmacy door

Every pharmacy posts the list of pharmacies on duty on its door. If you walk past one, take a photo: you'll have it on your iPhone when you need it.

## Why maps aren't enough

Apple Maps and Google Maps know each pharmacy's regular opening hours, not the duty rota, which changes every day. So a pharmacy on duty may show as closed, and vice versa.

Whichever way you choose, call before you set off: the rota sometimes changes at the last minute.

## In an emergency

If it's urgent, don't look for a pharmacy: call **166** (ambulance) or **112**, the European emergency number, which also answers in English.
