Every phone photo records when and where it was taken. Together, your photos are a complete travel diary that nobody has written down yet.

## Apple Photos

The Photos app on iPhone creates Memories and shows photos on a map. It's lovely, but it won't tell you how many countries you've visited, how far you travelled this year, or exactly when that trip to Vienna was. And it only sees photos in your Photos library, not the ones you keep in another cloud.

## With Apollo: trips that build themselves

[Apollo](/en/apollo/) reads the photos in your cloud and **detects your trips automatically** from their dates and locations. You just give them a name.

- Each trip has a map, its days and its photos.
- **Stats** for distance and countries, by year or overall.
- **Boarding passes and ferry tickets**: scan them with the camera or from a PDF, and they're filed under the right trip.
- **Reminders** before every upcoming trip.

## A small tip

Trips need photos with a location. If your camera had no GPS, add it in bulk with [Iris](/en/iris/) on your Mac. One search on the map is enough for all the photos of a trip.
