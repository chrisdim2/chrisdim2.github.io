"Photography" is a Greek word: phos, light, and graphein, to write. Every photo is, quite literally, written with light. So it made sense for a photo app to take the name of the god who came to be linked with light.

## Apollo in Greek myth

Apollo was the son of Zeus and Leto and the twin brother of Artemis. The god of music, poetry, prophecy and healing, he had at Delphi one of the most famous oracles of the ancient world.

## Phoebus, "the bright one"

Homer very often calls him "Phoebus Apollo". Phoebus is usually explained as "bright" or "shining" (the exact origin of the word is still debated). The name of Phoebe, his grandmother, shares the same root. In later centuries Apollo was linked more and more with light and the sun, and he was often identified with Helios, the sun god.

## Why it suits a photo app

If photography is writing with light, the "bright" god was the natural name. [Apollo](/en/apollo/) is a family photo album that works right on your own cloud: it shows the photos you already keep on pCloud, Dropbox, OneDrive or a NAS, with albums by year, a map and trips, without copying them anywhere.
