The coloured part of your eye, a flower and a rare metal have something in common: they all took their name from the same goddess.

## Iris in Greek myth

Iris was the messenger of the gods and the personification of the rainbow. A daughter of Thaumas and Electra, she carries the messages of Zeus and Hera in Homer. The rainbow was her road between heaven and earth.

## One name, three things

In ancient Greek, iris means rainbow. From there:

- The **iris of the eye** took the name for the many colours it can have.
- The **iris flower** did too, for the colours of its blooms.
- **Iridium**: the English chemist Smithson Tennant discovered it in 1803 and named it after Iris, because its salts took on strikingly different colours.

## Why it suits a photo app

Iris carried messages. Every photo carries its own, hidden inside the file: when it was taken, where, with which camera. [Iris](/en/iris/) is a Mac app that fixes dates and locations, writes titles and keywords, renames files and strips what you don't want to share. And just as the goddess linked heaven and earth, it gets your photos ready before they reach [Apollo](/en/apollo/).
