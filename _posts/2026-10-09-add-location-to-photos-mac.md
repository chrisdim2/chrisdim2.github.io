Phone photos have a location. Photos from a camera without GPS, or scans of old prints, don't. So they're missing from the map and from your trips, and a search for "Rome" never finds them.

## Inside Apple Photos

In Photos on a Mac, you open the **Info** window of a photo and type a location. For a few photos that's fine. For three hundred from one trip, or for files outside the library, it gets tedious.

## With Iris: one search for all of them

In [Iris](/en/iris/) you select the photos, search for the place on the map, say "Nafplio", and the location is written **inside each file**. From then on every app sees it: Photos, [Apollo](/en/apollo/), the Finder.

- See at once **which photos have no location**.
- Works on photos and **videos**.
- The place name is filled in automatically, using Apple's service.
- Every change can be **undone**.

## A small tip

If you had both a phone and a camera on a trip, sort by time: your phone photos tell you where you were each day, so you know what to give the camera photos.
