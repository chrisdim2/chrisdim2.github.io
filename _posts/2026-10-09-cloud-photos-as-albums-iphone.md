Many of us keep our photos outside iCloud: in pCloud with its lifetime plan, in Dropbox from work, in OneDrive that comes with Microsoft 365, or on a NAS at home. The problem is that there, photos are **files in folders**, not albums. To find "that summer on Naxos in 2019" you open a folder, then a subfolder, and wait for thumbnails to load.

## The usual fix, and why it's awkward

The first idea is to copy everything into the Photos app. But then you need iCloud storage, a second subscription for the same photos, and two copies that have to stay in sync.

## With Apollo: no copying at all

[Apollo](/en/apollo/) connects to your cloud (pCloud, Dropbox, OneDrive, Box, or WebDAV such as Nextcloud or a NAS) and shows your photos where they already are. It doesn't copy them anywhere.

- **Albums by year**, using the real capture dates from EXIF, not the upload date.
- A **map** of every photo that has a location.
- **Smart search**: type "sea" or "dog" and it finds them. The analysis runs on your device only, nothing is sent to third parties.
- It picks the best covers by itself and finds **duplicates**.

## A small tip

If many photos show up under the wrong year, the date inside the file is usually to blame, for example in scans or photos saved from WhatsApp. [Iris](/en/iris/) fixes it on your Mac before Apollo sees them.
