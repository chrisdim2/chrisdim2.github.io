Privacy Policy

App: Argus · Last updated: October 28, 2026

In short: Argus has no accounts, no servers of its own, collects no usage analytics and shows no ads. Your scans, the text read from them and your PDFs stay on your device. Text recognition happens on the device. We have no access to any of it.

1. Who is responsible

Controller: Christos Dimakis, Peristeri, Attica, Greece.
Contact: argus@dimakis.app

2. What the app uses

  • Camera (if you allow it): for scanning. The photos are stored only inside the app.
  • Photos: when you pick photos to scan, Argus sees only the ones you pick, through the system picker, with no access to the rest of your library. To “Save to Photos” it asks for add-only permission.
  • Files: images and PDFs that you choose to import.
  • Documents: pages, PDFs, the text read from them, folders and tags are stored on the device, inside the app. They are never sent anywhere.
  • Text recognition (OCR): happens on the device, with Tesseract and with Apple’s text recognition. No text or image is sent to a server.
  • Translation: happens on the device with Apple’s system translation. The first time for a language, iOS may ask you to download that language from Apple.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: for locked folders. iOS performs the authentication; the app only learns whether it succeeded. We have no access to biometric data.
  • PDF password: the password you lock a PDF with is used only for that file and is not stored anywhere.
  • Settings: your choices, your signatures and the page sizes you create are stored only on the device.
  • Sharing and printing: happen only when you ask, through the system. The app or recipient you choose (e.g. Mail, Messages, a cloud service) has its own policy.

3. Internet connection

Scanning, the tools and reading Greek and English work offline: the files for these two languages are inside the app. The app connects to the internet only in the cases below, always with plain requests that carry no information about you or your documents:

  • OCR languages: if you download another language in Settings, its file comes from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com, the tesseract-ocr project). As the server, GitHub sees your IP address.
  • Our apps: the list of our apps, with names, icons and links, is downloaded from our website (dimakis.app, hosted on GitHub Pages) when you open that page.
  • Privacy Policy and Terms of Use: open from dimakis.app in a Safari window inside the app.
  • Contact: opens your mail app with a message to us. At its end it includes support details (app version, device model, iOS version, language, region, time zone and OCR languages), which you can see and delete before sending. Nothing is sent unless you tap “Send” yourself.

4. What we don’t do

  • There is no account or sign-up.
  • There are no ads and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • We don’t track you across apps or websites and don’t use the IDFA.
  • We don’t sell or share data, because we don’t collect it.

5. Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to correct or delete on our side. You delete your documents inside the app; deleting the app deletes all of them. You can revoke any permission (Camera, Photos, Face ID) in the Settings of your iPhone or iPad. You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr) or the authority of your country.

6. Children

The app is not aimed specifically at children and collects no data from anyone.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the date at the top will be updated.
