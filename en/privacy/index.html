Privacy policy

For the dimakis.app website. Last updated: November 17, 2026.

In short: this site sets no cookies, shows no ads and doesn’t follow you around other sites. We count visits anonymously, without keeping your IP address. We only have your email address if you ask to be notified about a launch and confirm it, and we only use it for that.

This page covers the website only. Each app has its own privacy policy, linked from its page.

1. Who is responsible

Data controller: Christos Dimakis, Peristeri, Attica, Greece.
Contact: {{ site.data.site.contact_email }}

2. When you open a page

Whenever your browser requests a page, it sends, as with any website, your IP address, your browser and device type (User-Agent) and the page you came from. Two services see this, each for its own purpose:

  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub logs requests, including IP addresses, for security and to run the service, under its own privacy statement. We have no access to those logs.
  • Visit statistics: we use GoatCounter, a cookie-free analytics service. It records which page you opened, where you came from, your browser and operating system, your screen size and your country. The country is derived from your IP address, which is not stored. To avoid counting the same visit twice, an anonymous fingerprint (hash) that can’t be traced back to you is kept for a few hours. We only ever see totals, never individual visitors.

Videos, images and fonts are hosted on the site itself. We don’t embed YouTube, maps, share buttons or any other third-party content.

3. Launch notifications (email)

If you fill in the “Notify me” form, your email address is sent to Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France), the service that sends our emails. It is stored together with the app you’re interested in (or “all”) and the language of the page, so we only write to you about that app and in your language.

  • Confirmation: your sign-up only counts once you tap “Confirm” in the email you receive (double opt-in). Brevo keeps the date of your sign-up and confirmation as proof that you asked for it.
  • What you’ll get: one email on the day the app is released on the App Store. No advertising, and we never give or sell your address to anyone.
  • Unsubscribing: every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. You can also write to us at {{ site.data.site.contact_email }} and we’ll delete your address.
  • How long we keep it: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.
  • Email measurements: Brevo may record whether you opened an email and whether you clicked one of its links. We don’t use this for profiling or advertising.

Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time. See Brevo’s privacy policy.

4. When you email us

Emails to @dimakis.app addresses are forwarded through Cloudflare (Cloudflare Email Routing) to our mailbox. We only use them to reply to you and never share them. If you’d like our correspondence deleted, just ask.

5. Links to other sites

The site links to the App Store, our social profiles (Instagram, Threads, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok), Wikipedia and the Press Kit files, which download from GitHub. None of them load unless you click. Once you open one, that site’s privacy policy applies.

6. Cookies

This site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser (no localStorage or similar). That’s why you won’t see a cookie banner.

7. What we don’t do

  • No ads and no social media pixels (Meta, Google Ads, TikTok and the like).
  • We don’t track you across other sites or build visitor profiles.
  • We don’t sell or give data to anyone.
  • There are no user accounts on the site.

8. Legal basis

For hosting and anonymous statistics: our legitimate interest in running the site properly and securely and in knowing which pages are read (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). For launch notifications: your consent (section 3).

9. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to withdraw your consent. Write to us at {{ site.data.site.contact_email }}. Visit statistics are anonymous, so we can’t find anything in them that relates to you. You can also lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr) or the supervisory authority where you live.

10. Changes

If any of the above changes, this page and the date at the top will be updated.
