Privacy Policy

App: ECU Data View · Last updated: November 20, 2026

In short: ECU Data View has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads and no analytics. It reads live data from your engine ECU over Bluetooth, read only, and shows it on your screen. Everything the app keeps stays on your device. We receive nothing and have no access to any of it.

1. Who is responsible

Controller: Christos Dimakis, Peristeri, Attica, Greece.
Contact: support@dimakis.app

2. Where your data lives

  • Dashboard and settings. Your pages, gauges, colours, units and the car details you type (for example a name or model) are stored only in the app’s storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Recordings. If you record a run, the values read from your ECU during that run are saved in a database on your device only. You can delete them in the app, and deleting the app deletes them too.
  • Photos for the background or logo. If you choose a picture for the dashboard background or logo, it is kept only on your device.
  • Exports. If you export a recording as a file, the app creates it on your device and opens the system share sheet. Where it goes is your choice (for example Mail or Files); we do not receive it.

3. What the app uses

  • Bluetooth: to connect to your EMU Bluetooth module and read live data. The app is read only: it only receives data from the ECU and never writes to it.
  • Location (while the app is in use): only the speed reported by GPS, for the speed display and the performance timer when your ECU has no speed sensor. The app does not read or store your position and does not show a map.
  • Motion sensors: for the G-meter and the road grade. The values stay on the device.
  • Camera: only if you choose to take a photo for the dashboard background or logo. It is not used otherwise.
  • Contacts, Microphone, Photos library access beyond the picture you choose: not used.

4. Internet connection

The app does not connect to the internet by itself and sends no data to us or to anyone else.

  • If you tap “Find the specs” when entering a car name, the link opens a Google search in your browser. That is a normal web search made by you, under Google’s own privacy policy.
  • If you open the Privacy Policy, Terms of Use or support page from the App Store page, they open in your browser from dimakis.app, hosted on GitHub Pages. The app itself does not connect to dimakis.app.

5. What we don’t do

  • There is no account or sign-up with us.
  • There are no ads and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • We don’t track you across apps or websites and don’t use the IDFA.
  • We don’t read, analyse or use your data, nor to train any artificial intelligence system.
  • We don’t sell or share data, because we don’t collect it.

6. Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to correct or delete on our side. You can delete everything the app keeps by deleting your recordings and the app. You can revoke the Bluetooth, Location, Motion and Camera permissions in your system Settings. You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr) or the authority of your country.

7. Children

The app is a tool for car enthusiasts and is not aimed at children. It collects no data from anyone.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the date at the top will be updated.
