Privacy Policy

App: Iris (macOS) · Last updated: October 5, 2026

The short version: Iris has no accounts and no servers. It doesn’t collect analytics and doesn’t show ads. It works only on the files you choose or drag into it, and everything happens on your Mac. Your photos and videos never leave your computer. Iris goes online only for the map, place search, and the place name for the coordinates you’re viewing (all provided by Apple) and to open these pages from within the app (see section 6).

1. Who we are

Data controller: Christos Dimakis, Peristeri, Attica, Greece.
Contact: iris@dimakis.app

2. We don’t collect anything

Iris has no server. You don’t create an account or give us your email address, and the app contains no analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs. We don’t track you across apps or websites. None of your photos, videos, or metadata ever passes through our systems, because we don’t have any.

3. What Iris does

Iris gets your photos and videos ready before they go into your library: it fixes capture dates and locations, adds titles, descriptions, and keywords, renames files in bulk, and finds duplicates. It uses built-in macOS frameworks (ImageIO and AVFoundation) and runs in Apple’s App Sandbox, which means it can only access the files and folders you choose or drag into it.

4. Data that stays on your Mac

The following is stored locally and never sent anywhere:

CategoryWhat it isWhy
File accessmacOS security-scoped bookmarks for the files and folders you’ve chosenSo Iris keeps access to them between launches
Undo historyOne JSON file per batch, with each file’s path and its previous values (date, GPS, title, description, keywords, name)So you can undo changes. Iris keeps your last 50 batches and deletes older ones automatically. These files live in the app’s own storage (Application Support), not in your folders
Rename recovery recordThe file paths of a rename in progressSo files can be restored if the app quits unexpectedly. Deleted as soon as the rename finishes
Recent placesUp to 12 places you’ve used, with their names and coordinatesSo you don’t have to enter the same coordinates again
SettingsThe Subfolders and Verify options and your Language choiceTo remember how you like the app to work
Activity logThe last 500 log entries, kept in memory onlyTo show what happened, including any errors. Cleared when you quit the app
Temporary filesA temporary file and a backup copy, stored next to the file being savedTo save files safely. Deleted automatically once saving is complete
We don’t keep copies of your photos. Changes are made directly to your files, right where they are. If you delete Iris, its undo history and settings go with it — your files stay put.

5. Permissions

Iris doesn’t ask for access to your Photos library, camera, contacts, or location. The locations you see come from the files themselves (GPS data) or from what you pick on the map, never from where you are. The only files and folders Iris can access are the ones you choose in the Open dialog or drag into the app. Iris is allowed to connect to the internet only to load these pages (section 6); the map works through a macOS system service.

6. What leaves your Mac, and where it goes

ServiceWhat’s sentWhy
Apple Maps (MapKit)The map area you’re viewing and, when you click Search, the text you typed (e.g. “Chalkida”)To display the map and look up the coordinates of a place
Apple (place names)The coordinates of the file you select, or the ones you type. This happens automatically, without you clicking anythingTo show the place name (e.g. “Chalkida”) under the map, so you can tell right away whether the location is correct
The Iris website (dimakis.app, hosted on GitHub Pages)A single page request, like visiting any website, and only when you open the Privacy Policy or Terms of Use from Settings. The server sees your IP address and standard request detailsSo you always see the latest version of these pages. GitHub’s Privacy Statement also applies

That’s it. We never receive your files, their names, or their metadata, nor your name, email address, or any device identifier.

The map and place names tell Apple which spot you’re looking at. When you select a file that has GPS data, the map centers on that spot and Iris automatically asks Apple for the name of the place. Apple sees the coordinates — but not the photo itself or the file’s name. Apple’s Privacy Policy applies. If you’d rather avoid this, work offline: everything else still works, and you can enter coordinates by hand.

7. How long data is kept

We don’t keep anything, because we don’t have anything. On your Mac, data stays for as long as you want it. The undo history keeps your last 50 batches and deletes older ones automatically, and it’s removed entirely when you delete the app. Neither affects your photos.

8. Support

If you contact us through Settings → Contact, your email app opens with just the subject line filled in: the app’s name and version. Nothing else is attached — no file names, locations, or photos. We keep support emails only for as long as it takes to resolve your issue.

9. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, and port your data, and to object to its processing. Since we don’t hold any of your data, you exercise these rights directly on your Mac. For anything else, just email us. You also have the right to file a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr).

10. Children

Iris isn’t directed at children under 13 and doesn’t knowingly collect data from them. In fact, since it collects no data at all, there’s nothing to collect.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make any significant changes, we’ll update the date at the top and mention it in the release notes. You can always find this policy in the app, under Settings.
