Privacy Policy

App: Pluto · Last updated: November 18, 2026

In short: Pluto has no accounts, no servers of its own, collects no usage analytics and shows no ads. It reads the statistics and reviews of your apps straight from Apple, with your own App Store Connect key, and keeps them on your device. We have no access to any of it.

1. Who is responsible

Controller: Christos Dimakis, Peristeri, Attica, Greece.
Contact: pluto@dimakis.app

2. Where your data lives

  • Your App Store Connect key. The Issuer ID, the Key ID, the contents of the .p8 file and the vendor number you enter are stored only in the Keychain of your device. If iCloud Keychain is turned on, Apple syncs them to your other devices (iPhone and Mac) under your Apple Account and Apple’s own privacy policy; we receive nothing. “Disconnect” in Settings deletes them from the Keychain, and from iCloud Keychain too.
  • Statistics and reviews. Daily and yearly download figures, the list of your apps, their versions in the App Store (number, platform and state, for example “in review” or “rejected”, and the phased release), the TestFlight builds (version, build number, processing state and expiry date) and your customers’ reviews (the star rating, title, text, nickname, country and date that Apple provides, and your own replies) are downloaded from Apple and stored in a database on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Widget (iPhone). A small summary of yesterday’s downloads is shared between the app and its widget on the same device, through an App Group. It does not leave the device.
  • Settings. Your choices (for example which AI features are on) are stored only on the device.

3. What the app uses

  • Notifications (if you allow them): a notification when a new review of one of your apps arrives, when one of their versions changes state in the App Store (in review, approved, rejected, released) and when a TestFlight build is ready, fails or is about to expire. They are local notifications created on your device. There is no push server. To find new reviews, versions and builds while the app is closed, iOS may wake the app now and then in the background, and the app asks Apple for them as described in section 4.
  • On-device AI (optional, you can turn it off in Settings): the summary of reviews and the draft of a reply use Apple’s Foundation Models framework on devices with Apple Intelligence. The review text is processed on the device and is not sent to us or to any other server. On other devices, and for languages the model does not support, the app uses built-in rules instead, with the same privacy.
  • Camera, Microphone, Location, Contacts, Photos: the app does not use them.

4. Internet connection

The app connects to the internet only to talk to Apple, with your own key, and only to the addresses below. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

  • api.appstoreconnect.apple.com: the App Store Connect API. The app signs each request with a short-lived token made on your device from your key, and asks for your apps, your sales and download reports, and your customer reviews. When you send a reply to a review, the text of the reply is sent to this address, only after you have seen a preview and tapped Send. Apple handles these requests under its own privacy policy.
  • Apple’s image servers (mzstatic.com): to show the icon of each of your apps, the app downloads it from Apple’s image servers, using the address Apple gives for the app’s latest build. This request carries no key, token or account information. As the server, Apple sees the IP address of your device.
  • Privacy Policy, Terms of Use and support: if you open them from the App Store page, they open in your browser from dimakis.app, hosted on GitHub Pages. The app itself does not connect to dimakis.app.

5. What we don’t do

  • There is no account or sign-up with us.
  • There are no ads and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • We don’t track you across apps or websites and don’t use the IDFA.
  • We don’t read, analyse or use your data or your customers’ reviews, nor to train any artificial intelligence system.
  • We don’t sell or share data, because we don’t collect it.

6. Your customers’ reviews

Reviews contain personal data of your customers (for example a nickname). Pluto shows them to you only because you are the developer of the app they were written for, and keeps them only on your device. If you use them (for example by quoting them), you are responsible for doing so lawfully.

7. Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to correct or delete on our side. You can delete everything the app keeps by tapping “Disconnect” and deleting the app. You can revoke the Notifications permission in your system Settings. You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr) or the authority of your country.

8. Children

The app is a tool for developers and is not aimed at children. It collects no data from anyone.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the date at the top will be updated.
