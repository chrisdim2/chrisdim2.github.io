Privacy Policy

App: Apollo · Last updated: 24 September 2026

In short: Apollo has no accounts, no usage analytics and no ads, and we keep no data about you. Your photos stay where they already are, in your own cloud account, and your trips and boarding passes stay on your device. Two small helper services of ours exist only for technical reasons (Box sign-in and Wallet cards); they store nothing (section 2).

2. What we collect: nothing we keep

You do not create an account with us, you do not give us an email address, and there is no analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDK embedded. We do not track you across apps or websites and we do not use the IDFA. No photo and no video ever passes through a system of ours.

There are exactly two small helper services, run by us on Cloudflare. Each one exists because something cannot be done safely from inside the app:

3. What Apollo is

Apollo is a viewer for photos you already keep in your own cloud: pCloud, Dropbox, OneDrive, Box, WebDAV, or any S3-compatible service, or your device's Photos library. The app connects directly from your device to the service you chose. You can also transfer photos directly from one of your own accounts to another (see section 6).

It also keeps your trips: flights and ferry journeys, and the boarding passes for them, which you enter yourself or scan from the ticket.

5. Permissions the app requests

Each is asked for only when you first use the feature that needs it, and each is optional.

  • Photos: two uses. First, if you connect your device's Photos library as a source, to read and display it. Second, to save photos you download or transfer from a cloud into it.
  • Camera: to scan the barcode on a boarding pass so the flight details fill in by themselves. Recognition happens on the device; no image is stored or sent.
  • Calendar: write-only access, to add a trip to your calendar when you ask. The app cannot read your calendar.
  • Notifications: reminders before a departure. They are scheduled on the device; nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Background processing: so syncing and EXIF reading can continue while the app is closed.

The app does not request location permission. The locations you see on the map come from your own files (EXIF GPS), not from where you are. Every permission can be revoked at any time in the system Settings.

Transferring between your own accounts. If you choose to transfer photos from one of your connected accounts to another (e.g. from your device's library to pCloud, or from Dropbox to OneDrive), the file is downloaded from the first and uploaded to the second directly through your device. 7. Connecting your cloud accounts

Sign-in uses OAuth, the standard that lets an app get access without ever seeing your password. You give the password directly to the provider. The app receives only an access token, kept in your device's Keychain, which you can revoke at any time:

  • inside the app, Settings → the provider → Disconnect,
  • or from the provider's own security settings.

Box is the one exception to "directly". Box requires a secret for sign-in that must not be stored inside an app, so that one step goes through our helper service (section 2), which forwards it without keeping anything. For WebDAV and S3-compatible services you enter the credentials yourself; they are kept in the Keychain.

The providers' policies apply: pCloud, Apple, Dropbox, Microsoft (OneDrive), Box, or the policy of your own WebDAV/S3-compatible provider.

8. Family mode

If you enable it, the app writes two small files inside the photo folder of the cloud account you choose as the family hub (pCloud unless you pick another of your connected accounts), so that anyone with access to the same folder can read them:

  • .photoapp-family.json: album covers, year groupings, place names, the member names you define, your trips (name, dates, who went, transport, expenses and ratings), your flights and ferry journeys, your boarding passes (passenger name, seat, booking code and barcode) and the list of shared photos moved to the trash.
  • .photoapp-exif.json: capture dates, GPS coordinates and camera models, so that each device does not have to read them again.

The boarding passes are shared on purpose: one person scans everyone's tickets and each member finds their own on their own phone.

These files contain location data, people's names and boarding passes. Anyone with access to the shared folder can read them, and a booking code with a barcode can be enough to view or change a booking. "Members" are plain labels you type yourself, not accounts, and there is no sign-in. Enable this feature only with people you already trust with your photos and your tickets. You can turn it off, or keep only part of it (for example without trips), in Settings → Family mode. The files live in your own cloud account, not with us, and you can delete them whenever you want.

The "I am…" setting (which member you are on this device) and the choice of family hub are not synced; they stay on the device only.

9. How long data is kept

By us, not at all, because we hold nothing: the helper services keep nothing after replying. On your device, as long as you want. You can clear thumbnails and temporary files from Settings, delete trips and passes, disconnect a provider (which also removes its entries from the index), or uninstall the app, which deletes everything. Your photos in the cloud are not affected.

10. Support

If you write to us from Help → Contact, the message is pre-filled with technical details that help diagnosis: app version, system version, device model, which clouds are connected, whether Family mode is on, number of photos in the index, free space and language. No file names, locations, photos, trips or credentials are included. You see them before sending and can delete them. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve the issue.

11. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Because we hold none of your data, these rights are exercised directly on your device and in your cloud accounts. For anything else, write to us. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr) or your local supervisory authority.

12. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Since we keep no data at all, no such risk exists.
