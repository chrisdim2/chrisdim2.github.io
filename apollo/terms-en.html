1. What the app is

Apollo is a viewer and organiser for photos and videos you already keep in your own cloud (pCloud) or in your Photos library (iCloud Photos). It shows a timeline, albums by year, a location map and search, by reading your files' metadata. It is not a storage service and it does not replace your backups.

The app modifies your files. When you correct a date or location and ask for it to be written into the files, when you crop or apply filters to a photo, or when you trim and merge videos, the original file in the cloud is replaced. Deleting from the app is a permanent deletion from the cloud. There is no trash inside the app. Keep backups before performing bulk operations.

3. Dates and locations

The app tries to find the real capture date by reading the files' EXIF, the file name and the year folder. When those are missing or wrong, the date shown is an estimate. The same applies to the "estimate location from nearby shots" feature, which borrows coordinates from photos taken close in time. Estimates are marked separately inside the app and you can confirm or undo them. Do not treat them as evidence.

4. Family mode

If you enable it, the app writes settings files inside the shared pCloud folder, and those files include capture dates and GPS coordinates of your photos. Anyone with access to that folder can read them. It is your responsibility to enable the feature only in folders you share with people you trust. See also the Privacy Policy, section 8.
