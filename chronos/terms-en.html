1. What the app is

Chronos is a personal calendar with organisation features: events and reminders, weather, countdowns, medication reminders, tracking of maintenance and subscriptions, the Greek name-day calendar, and information about on-duty pharmacies and hospitals in Greece.

This app is not a medical device. Medication reminders are an organisational tool, not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. They do not replace the instructions of your doctor or pharmacist. Never change your treatment based on what the app shows. In an emergency call 112, or 166 for the Greek ambulance service.
