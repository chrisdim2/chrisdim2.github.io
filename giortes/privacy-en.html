Privacy Policy

App: Chronos Celebrations · Last updated: October 23, 2026

In short: Chronos Celebrations has no accounts, no servers of its own, collects no usage analytics and shows no ads. The names and birthdays of your contacts are read only on your device and never leave it.

1. Who is responsible

Controller: Christos Dimakis, Peristeri, Attica, Greece.
Contact: giortes@dimakis.app

2. What the app uses

  • Contacts (if you allow it): names, birthdays and thumbnails. They are read on the device to show which contacts have a name day or a birthday, and for notifications. They are never sent anywhere or stored outside the device. We do not modify your contacts.
  • Notifications (if you allow it): local notifications scheduled on the device. No push servers are used.
  • Settings: your choices (notification times, language, holiday country, theme, icon) are stored only on the device.
  • Device region: the region set on your iPhone decides the default country for public holidays (Greece or Cyprus). We do not use GPS location.

3. Internet connection

For the “On this day” section and the saints’ calendar, the app asks Wikipedia (wikipedia.org, Wikimedia Foundation) for the page of the day. The request contains the date you are viewing and an app identifier (name, version and the contact address stated here). No contacts or other personal data are sent. As the server, the Wikimedia Foundation sees your IP address and applies its own privacy policy. Name days, public holidays, Easter, the moon phase and zodiac signs are calculated on the device without a network.

4. What we do not do

  • There is no account or sign-up.
  • There are no ads and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites and we do not use the IDFA.
  • We do not sell or share data, because we do not collect it.

5. Your rights

Because we do not keep personal data about you, there is nothing for us to correct or delete. You can revoke any permission (Contacts, Notifications) in iPhone Settings, or delete the app. You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr).

6. Children

The app is not aimed at children in particular and does not collect data from anyone.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the date at the top will be updated.
